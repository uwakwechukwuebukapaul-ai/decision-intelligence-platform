# Sentinel DNA — Founder Notes
**Date:** August 4, 2026

## Mission

Build the world's most trusted AI-native SOC Investigation Platform that helps security teams investigate faster, understand threats more deeply, and respond with confidence.

---

## Current Focus

**DO NOT chase new shiny features.**

Our priority is building a stable, enterprise-ready investigation platform.

Current objectives:

- Complete the current milestone cleanly.
- Strengthen the AI investigation pipeline.
- Improve architecture and maintainability.
- Remove technical debt.
- Keep the platform modular and scalable.
- Maintain backward compatibility where practical.

---

## Founder Rules

- Product before hype.
- Quality before speed.
- Architecture before shortcuts.
- Real customer problems before trendy features.
- Every release must improve the investigation experience.

---

## Decision Filter

Before building anything, ask:

1. Does this help analysts investigate faster?
2. Does it improve investigation quality?
3. Will this still make sense when Sentinel DNA has 100,000 users?
4. Does it strengthen our long-term competitive advantage?

If the answer is **No**, it goes into the backlog.
